About

I studied Computer Science at Maseno University and graduated in 2023. I started out building websites and mobile apps, then moved into data during an internship at Ramana Tech School, where I built a customer 360 view from bank transaction data with MongoDB and Apache Spark, shown in Streamlit. Since then I have worked with an FMCG distributor and a liquor distributor on forecasting, pricing and order data. I am looking for data science roles in banking and retail.

What I am learning now

How to evaluate LLM output properly. Matching supplier products and designing the analytics assistant showed me that the hard part is knowing when the model is wrong, so I am building labelled test sets and scoring methods for matching and extraction tasks. After the Zindi challenge I am also going further into quantisation and running small models on modest hardware.
